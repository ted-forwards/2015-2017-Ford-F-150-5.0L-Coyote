# Preventative maintenance through 300,000 km
Conservative owner-selected plan, not factory schedule. Follow VIN-specific manual, oil-life monitor and severe-duty intervals.
| Interval | Tasks |
|---|---|
| Purchase | Verify VIN, recalls, cooler line repair and fluid level; scan modules; inspect tires, brakes, frame and steering |
| 8,000 km / 6 months | Oil/filter (earlier if indicated), tire rotation as appropriate, inspect leaks, belts, brakes |
| Annual | Rustproofing; inspect frame, brake/fuel lines, IWE, CV boots, hubs, joints, cooling, battery, exhaust |
| 50,000 km recurring | Review differential, transfer case and transmission fluid needs; coolant condition |
| 100,000 km | Review plugs, belts, hoses, brake fluid and drivetrain service history |
| 150,000 km | Review plugs and transmission filter/fluid; inspect pump, hubs, struts and seals |
| 200,000 km | Comprehensive suspension, cooling, starting/charging and exhaust inspection |
| 250,000 km | Baseline inspection; assess oil consumption, corrosion and drivetrain wear |
| 300,000 km | Full condition assessment; continue recurring intervals |
Do not replace components solely because another truck needed them at a particular mileage. Use correct fluid specifications and level procedure.
