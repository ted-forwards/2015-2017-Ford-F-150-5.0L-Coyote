# Known issues and recalls
| Concern | Symptoms / action |
|---|---|
| Cooler lines | Fluid leak; inspect connections and verify level after repair |
| 6R80 lead frame / range sensing | Unexpected shifts, warning lights; scan and consult Ford |
| IWE vacuum system | Grinding in 2WD; inspect vacuum lines, check valve and actuators |
| Cooling | Coolant loss or abnormal temperature; pressure test pump, thermostat and hoses |
| Front suspension | Clunks, play, uneven tire wear; inspect hubs, arms, joints, struts and CV boots |
| Starting/charging | Slow crank, no start, warning lights; test battery, wiring, starter and alternator |
| Corrosion | Inspect frame, brake/fuel lines and exhaust |
## Recall records
26S28 unexpected downshift/PCM: 10 reported open; two additional PCM programming records with campaign unconfirmed. 24S77 wiper linkage: 4 open. 17S33 door latch: 1 open. 18S45 block heater wiring: 1 open; avoid affected heater until remedy verified. Three reports lack recall information.
