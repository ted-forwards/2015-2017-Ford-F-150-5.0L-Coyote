## Research snapshot
13 vehicles, highest documented service mileage 235,011 km. No documented complete engine replacement or transmission rebuild. This is a convenience sample, not a population failure-rate study. Some engines and model years are unverified; one record is an independent inspection rather than a complete CARFAX history.

Keep personal VINs, invoices and completed maintenance logs outside this public repository. Manufacturer campaign status must be checked by VIN. Costs are planning estimates in CAD.
