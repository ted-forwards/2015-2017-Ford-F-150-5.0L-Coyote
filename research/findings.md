## Mileage patterns
50–75k: thermostat, heater, wiper switch. 75–100k: brakes, alternator, wiring. 100–150k: coils, axles, suspension, cooler lines, hubs. 150–175k: pump, arms, struts, seal, fuel sensor. 175–200k: brakes, thermostat/housing. 200–250k: tailpipe, starter.
## Method
Convenience sample of resale listings and one independent inspection. Deduplicate vehicles; separate repairs, inspections, maintenance and possible campaigns. Uncertain mileage is bounded rather than imputed. Some engine specifications unverified. No exposure-adjusted failure rates.
